# Claims vs. Evidence Matrix

This matrix maps every central empirical and theoretical claim made by Phyu et al. (*IEEE TNSM* 2023) against the evidence presented in the paper, accompanied by our independent audit findings.

---

| Claim ID | Paper Claim | Source in Paper | Evidence Provided in Paper | Our Audit & Evaluation Verdict | Gap / Limitation Identified |
|:---:|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **C-01** | FPLSTM achieves accuracy on par with centralized DeepCog while preserving privacy. | Abstract, Sec I, Sec VI-E | Figure 7: FPLSTM RMSE is within 0.005–0.008 of DeepCog for FB/Google/IG, and better for YouTube. | **Partially Supported.** DeepCog was trained on capacity estimation with asymmetric loss, whereas FPLSTM minimized RMSE. Comparing them solely on RMSE favors FPLSTM. | No confidence intervals, single seed, no statistical significance test ($p$-value). |
| **C-02** | FedProx effectively manages network statistical and data heterogeneity over FedAvg. | Section IV-C, Sec VI-G | Figure 12: FPLSTM achieves lower RMSE than FALSTM across $\mu \in [0.001, 1.0]$. | **Supported, but implementation discrepancy exists.** Algorithm 2 implements a post-SGD step rather than in-loop proximal descent. | The magnitude of gain is modest (~0.002 to 0.004 RMSE improvement). |
| **C-03** | FPLSTM provides superior communication efficiency compared to centralized training. | Section VI-F, Figure 9(a) | Communication model $2 C N \Omega \psi$ gives a constant cost regardless of dataset fraction. | **Misleading Framing.** Over a 10-day period, transmitting raw traffic consumes only $\sim 1.3\text{ MB}$, whereas 20 FL rounds consume $\sim 628\text{ MB}$. FL is only cheaper asymptotically. | Ignored downlink broadcast mechanisms and initial synchronization costs. |
| **C-04** | FPLSTM delivers 9x to 18x better CPU time utilization than DeepCog. | Section VI-F, Figure 11 | CPU hours on Compute Canada: DeepCog required up to 18x more CPU time than FPLSTM. | **Implementation-Dependent.** DeepCog is a 3D-CNN; FPLSTM is a lightweight 1D-LSTM. Comparing unoptimized 3D-CNN CPU training against 1D-LSTM is an architectural mismatch, not an inherent FL property. | Distributed parallel training was compared against single-threaded centralized training without normalizing for total core-hours. |
| **C-05** | IC-FPLSTM clustering enables scalability, cutting CPU time from 65h to 15h. | Section VI-I, Figure 15 | Figure 15 shows CPU time curve scaling for 228 agents: FPLSTM takes 65h, IC-FPLSTM takes 15h. | **Partially Supported.** Clustering divides the 228 clients into sub-federations, reducing per-cluster client numbers. | CPU time calculation omits the computational overhead of running PCA and $k$-means iterations before training. |
| **C-06** | IC-FPLSTM improves accuracy over unclustered FPLSTM. | Section VI-I, Figure 14 | Figure 14 reports improvement $\alpha$ of 0.4% to 1.5% in RMSE for traffic-trend IC-FPLSTM. | **Marginal Significance.** A 0.4% to 1.5% RMSE gain is within standard random seed variance for neural networks. | Without error bars across multiple seeds, this gain may be statistical noise rather than genuine algorithmic superiority. |
| **C-07** | Random clustering (RC-FPLSTM) matches IC-FPLSTM without requiring traffic trends. | Section VI-I, Figure 14 | RC-FPLSTM RMSE is virtually identical to IC-FPLSTM across all 4 slices. | **Strong Contradiction to Paper's Hypothesis.** If random clustering matches trend clustering, the physical rationale for temporal/spatial clustering is challenged. | Exposes Algorithm 4 surplus-handling bug and questions the value of information-based clustering. |
| **C-08** | Global model provides effective cold-start mitigation for new slices. | Section VI-J, Figure 16 | Figure 16 shows IC-FPLSTM global model RMSE is ~6% lower than FPLSTM global model. | **Valid Concept, Thin Evidence.** Only 4 slices tested; cold-start performance on an unseen, unrepresented slice was not experimentally evaluated. | No ablation testing the transferability of the global model to an out-of-distribution vertical slice. |
